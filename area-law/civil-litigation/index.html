How we help

We will engage highly experienced and qualified solicitors and barristers to represent you, ensuring your case is managed by professionals with a proven track record.

From the outset, we provide you with clear and comprehensive written advice regarding the prospects of your case.

Transparent costs

We are transparent about fees from the outset.

  1. An initial consultationOur services begin with an initial consultation with a qualified solicitor. This gives you the opportunity to present the details of your case.
  2. Preliminary advice and an estimateWe will then offer preliminary advice along with a clear estimate of the likely costs involved.
  3. Payment by instalmentsWhere necessary, we can arrange for payment to be made in instalments over the course of the case.

Our published fee information is on the Our fees page of the current website (opens in a new tab) ↗.
