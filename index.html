02About Us

About Legal Comfort Solicitors

Legal Comfort Solicitors are a firm of solicitors based in London, Colchester, Hadleigh and Braintree who have positive attitudes and are professionally experienced. Our Solicitors offer expertise, understanding and knowledge. We are based in the centre of Colchester town centre.

Our law firm provide legal services and litigation support to individuals and corporate clients. When you become our client we will provide regular feedback and updates to you.

Clients are at the heart of everything that we do, we listen thoroughly to everything that the clients speaks and we make sure that we always give exceptional customer service. Our clients trust us to give the best and most honest legal advice in a friendly and understandable way.
