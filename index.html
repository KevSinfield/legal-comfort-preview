03About Legal Comfort

Your situation is personal. So is our approach.

Legal Comfort Solicitors is a firm of solicitors based in London, Colchester, Hadleigh and Braintree. We provide legal services and litigation support to individuals and corporate clients.

When you become our client we will provide regular feedback and updates to you. Our clients trust us to give the best and most honest legal advice in a friendly and understandable way.

Mr Javed Khalil Patel, Managing Director

04Our approach

A considered approach, from the first conversation.

We are transparent about fees from the outset.

01

An initial consultation

Our services begin with an initial consultation with a qualified solicitor. This gives you the opportunity to present the details of your case.

02

Advice and a clear estimate

We will offer preliminary advice along with a clear estimate of the likely costs involved.

03

Regular updates

Where necessary, we can arrange for payment in instalments over the course of the case, and we keep you updated throughout.
